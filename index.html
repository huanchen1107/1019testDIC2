🎯 Why Feature Selection?
More features do not always mean a better model! Adding redundant or noisy predictors leads to overfitting and high variance. Feature selection finds the optimal parsimonious subset that minimizes validation error.
📐 3 Selection Strategies
  • SFS (Forward): Starts empty; greedily adds the single feature that most reduces RMSE.
  • RFE (Backward): Starts full; recursively eliminates the feature with smallest importance.
  • Exhaustive: Evaluates all $2^N - 1$ combinations to guarantee the global optimum.
📏 Metrics Breakdown
  • RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): Penalizes large errors; lower is better.
  • MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average absolute deviation in target units.
  • $R^2$ (R-squared): Proportion of variance explained; 1.0 is perfect fit.
